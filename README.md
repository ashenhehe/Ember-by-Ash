# Ember by Ash

Light around your screen that moves with your music, synced lyrics that light up word by word, and a full-screen Music mode. For Windows 10 and 11.

## Download

**[Download Ember-Setup.exe](https://github.com/ashenhehe/Ember-by-Ash/raw/main/Ember-Setup.exe)**, run it, and press Install.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because Ember isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Ember updates itself: when a new version is out, it shows up in Settings and in the tray.

## What it does

- Edge light in your album's colours, with 10 styles and beat effects
- Synced lyrics with 19 styles, fonts, emoji and glow icons
- Music mode: cover styles, layouts and animated backgrounds
- Record 9:16 or landscape videos of your music with sound
- Spotify library: search, playlists and Liked Songs (needs Spotify Premium)
- Listening stats, live covers, album wallpaper

Works with Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, SoundCloud and anything that shows up in Windows' media controls.
